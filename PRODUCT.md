# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Industrial engineers, calibration specialists, procurement teams, plant managers, and operations directors. B2B buyers who research vendors before engaging. They expect technical credibility, specific capability claims, and proof of competence. Time is money; they scan for signal, not marketing filler.

## Product Purpose

Real Technologies is a solutions provider for industrial weighing, calibration, fabrication, and automation. The website must establish authority across four distinct service lines, surface the right entry point for each buyer type, and convert technical decision-makers who have already self-qualified via the content.

## Brand Personality

Precise, structured, authoritative. Engineering-document clarity with architectural presence. Not warm and fuzzy, not flashy. Competence expressed through restraint and precision.

## Anti-references

Generic corporate SaaS: blue gradients, stock-photo people in hardhats, "innovative solutions" copy, centered hero stacks. Outdated brochure sites: thin blue borders, Times New Roman, PDF-download CTAs. Neither.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the grid.** Structure is the brand. Asymmetric layouts, deliberate whitespace, and visible precision signal engineering competence.
2. **Specificity over generality.** Every headline must name the capability, not abstract the service. "Suspension Bridge Weighing Systems" beats "Industrial Solutions."
3. **Proof before assertion.** Credibility through numbers, specs, and concrete use cases, not testimonials with empty praise.
4. **No decorative motion.** Movement is functional or it is absent. Scroll reveals are earned by value, not applied for energy.
5. **Depth through contrast.** Dense technical information balanced against generous white space. The breathing room makes the data readable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Sufficient contrast on all text. Reduced motion respected. No information conveyed by color alone. The site serves a professional B2B audience including older engineers who may have vision changes.
